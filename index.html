Understanding the human brain

The Neuroimaging Group at the Cajal Neuroscience Center of the Spanish National Research Council (CSIC) investigates the human brain and its functioning, in health and across neurological conditions.

Our research Join or collaborate
{% include signal.html %}

Our objective is to characterize the brain in healthy populations and to study the changes occurring across various neurological conditions. We lead open science initiatives by sharing software, data repositories and contributing to neuroimaging standards and good practices.

We use a range of neuroimaging techniques: electroencephalography (EEG), magnetoencephalography (MEG), magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), functional MRI (fMRI), tractography (DTI), positron emission tomography (PET) and near-infrared spectroscopy (NIRS).
