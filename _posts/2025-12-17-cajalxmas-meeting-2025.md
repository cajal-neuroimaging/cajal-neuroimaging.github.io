El grupo presenta cinco pósteres en el XIII Cajal Xmas Meeting, el primero celebrado en el Centro de Neurociencias Cajal. Guiomar Niso coorganiza el encuentro y el II Certamen de Neurociencia y Arte.

**Development of a reproducible pipeline for analysis of resting-state EEG data.** López N., Reina J., Martínez N., Niso G.

**Motion-related artifacts in wireless EEG: characterizing auditory oddball ERPs with the Neurosity CROWN across posture and movement conditions.** Ponce J.P., Obeso I., Niso G.

**Habitual inhibitory control: behavioural and EEG evidence.** Sobrio L., Navarro M., Obeso I., Niso G.

**Automatic detection of high-frequency oscillations and interictal epileptiform discharges in drug-resistant epilepsy patients.** Martín Díez D., Salinas C., Toledano R., Niso G.

**EEG-based brain clocks for healthy aging: spectral characterization with an Alzheimer's disease comparison and plasma proteomics.** Reina J., López N., dos Santos Brandão Q., Pignatelli J., Niso G.
